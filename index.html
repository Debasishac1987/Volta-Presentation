Why We Exist

Mission & Goals

"One vision, two missions" — accelerating progress and ensuring quality delivery on both Legacy SkyApp and the evolving CELP framework.

Vision

Build customer trust with a seamless driver experience on telematics devices by enhancing features, adding stability, leveraging advanced tech stacks, and building best-in-class solutions.

Mission - Legacy (SkyApp)

Ensure uninterrupted service and progressive improvements to critical SkyApp modules — ECO, Planning, Tachograph, Reefer, Activity Manager — sustaining business continuity and user trust.

Mission - Nova (CELP)

Enable seamless transition of SkyApp functionality into CELP micro-services, focusing on modularity, performance, and ease of integration with future platforms.

What We Own

Modules & Features

Clear separation of ownership between Legacy SkyApp and CELP framework.

SkyApp Legacy Modules
  • ECO (Eco-Driving) — Real-time driver behavior scoring and feedback for fuel efficiency
  • Planning & Navigation — Route planning, trip management, and on-device navigation
  • Tachograph — Digital tachograph data download, display, and compliance management
  • Reefer (Refrigeration) — Temperature monitoring and control for cold-chain transport
  • Activity Manager — Driver activity tracking, work-rest timers, and compliance alerts
  • Messaging & Workflow — Two-way messaging and task management between driver and dispatch
CELP Next-Gen Services
  • AIS140 — India-specific GPS compliance service with VLTD protocol support
  • ECO Service — Re-architected eco-driving as a standalone CELP micro-service
  • Tacho Service — Tachograph data handling migrated to distributed, scalable architecture
  • RDD (Remote Data Download) — Remote tachograph data retrieval over-the-air
  • Drive-State — Real-time vehicle and driver state aggregation service
  • Activity Manager — Next-gen driver activity as a composable CELP service
